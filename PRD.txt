# 📄 Product Requirements Document (PRD)  
**Product Name:** Meet & Eat  
**Tagline:** *Connect & Learn from Someone New — Every Month.*

---

## 1. 🎯 Vision
Meet & Eat is a global networking platform that connects people randomly once a month to learn about each other’s **culture, work, and lifestyle**. It’s not about making friends or small talk — it’s about **sharing perspectives** and **breaking formal barriers** through authentic conversations.

---

## 2. 💡 Purpose
To help users **expand their network**, **share experiences**, and **learn from diverse individuals** worldwide — without the cognitive load of choosing who to connect with.

---

## 3. 👥 Target Audience
- Open to everyone: students, founders, HR professionals, activists, artists, etc.  
- Random pairing ensures diversity and surprise.  
- No moderators — the system fosters organic, respectful communication.

---

## 4. 🔑 Differentiation
- **Random monthly pairing** removes bias and effort in choosing connections.  
- **Intentional networking:** both users know they’re connecting to learn — no ghosting or cold calls.  
- **Surprise reveal:** seasonal themes make the experience playful and engaging.  
- **Email‑driven model:** one secure email per month with partner details and opt‑out options.

---

## 5. 🖥️ Platform Scope
- **Website (m‑site + desktop)** — responsive design.  
- **No mobile app login required** — users register once via LinkedIn.  
- **All communication via email** — website acts as onboarding and summary hub.

---

## 6. 📧 Communication Policy
- **One email per month only.**  
- Email includes:  
  - Partner’s name, LinkedIn profile, preferred language, and interests.  
  - Option to opt out for next month or permanently.  
  - Secure, phishing‑free link to connect.  

---

## 7. 🧭 User Flow (Website)
| Step | Description |
|------|--------------|
| **LinkedIn Sign‑In** | OAuth login via LinkedIn only. |
| **Profile Setup** | Ask for preferred language, interests (tags), and fun bio. |
| **Monthly Match Reveal** | “Your [Month] Match is Ready!” with seasonal animation and reveal button. |
| **Match Info** | Show partner’s name, LinkedIn, languages, and “Have you already connected?” prompt. |
| **Home Screen** | Default view showing monthly match reveal; bottom tray navigation: Settings | Monthly Match | All Connects. |
| **All Connects** | List of past matches with “Connect Again?” and “Report Mis‑Event.” |
| **Settings** | Manage email frequency (fixed at 1/month), take a break, or leave program. |

---

## 8. 🎨 Design Guidelines
- **Style:** Minimalist, warm, globally inclusive.  
- **Tone:** Friendly, fun, trustworthy, playful + professional.  
- **Color Palette:** Soft blues and coral accents.  
- **Logo:** Globe + plate motif (symbolizing connection over conversation).  
- **Typography:** Sans‑serif, clean and accessible.  
- **Animations:** Lightweight seasonal GIFs (e.g., sunset, autumn leaves).  
- **Layout:** Mobile‑first responsive grid.  

---

## 9. 🔒 Security & Compliance
- LinkedIn OAuth only (no other login).  
- Encrypted storage for LinkedIn email ID, password, and personal email ID (with consent).  
- GDPR‑compliant, phishing‑free communication.  
- Secure opt‑out links in every email.  
- Scalable for millions of users.

---

## 10. ⚙️ Technical Requirements
| Component | Technology |
|------------|-------------|
| **Frontend** | React.js / Next.js (responsive web design) |
| **Backend** | Node.js / Express |
| **Database** | PostgreSQL (encrypted fields) |
| **Authentication** | LinkedIn OAuth |
| **Email Service** | SendGrid / AWS SES |
| **Hosting** | Azure / AWS |
| **Security** | HTTPS, SSL, OAuth 2.0, encrypted tokens |

---

## 11. 📊 Metrics (First Month)
Track only for analysis:  
- Number of sign‑ups.  
- Email open rate.  
- Match reveal click‑through rate.  
- Connection confirmations.  
- Opt‑out rate.
